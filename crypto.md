simultaneously — check the actual price rather than trusting either
headline.

- **Difficulty**: 759,209,177,777 (xmrchain.net)
- **Mempool**: 23 pending transactions at time of check
- **Per-block tx counts**: ranged 0-76 across the last 11 blocks sampled,
  mostly 2-36
- No explorer surfaced an aggregated daily transaction total directly;
  rough extrapolation from block time (~2 min/block, ~720 blocks/day) and
  sampled per-block counts puts daily volume in the ballpark of
  10,000-15,000 tx/day — this is an estimate, not a reported figure.

Monero's ring signatures and confidential amounts mean explorers can't
show real sender/receiver addresses or transaction amounts — only counts
and network-level stats. That's the coin's core design property, not a
data-availability gap.
